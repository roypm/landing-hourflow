A closer look at the app

A clear summary, ready to share

PDF and JPEG exports include the month's hours, extras and total pay. Files are named to stay in order, for example 2026-08_Restaurante_Joan.pdf.

Your data stays on your device

HourFlow is designed for one person and works offline. You only need an internet connection to check for updates.

What stays on your phone

Jobs, shifts, rates, extras and templates are stored on your phone. No account or analytics.

What leaves, when you choose

A JSON backup, or a monthly summary as PDF or JPEG. Export a backup from Settings before installing an APK manually.

Language and appearance

Choose Spanish, English or Catalan, and follow your system theme or use light or dark mode.

Current version

HourFlow 1.1.0

Weekly and monthly templates, PDF and JPEG summaries, and JSON backups that work with older versions. The APK is about 8.4 MB.

HourFlow does not update automatically. Export your JSON backup before installing. If Android rejects the update because it was signed with a different key, uninstall the existing app, install the new APK, then import your backup.
